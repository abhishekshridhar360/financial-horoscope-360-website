Trust & FAQ

Before you begin.

Clear answers to the questions people naturally ask before starting their Financial Horoscope™.

What exactly is Financial Horoscope™?

Financial Horoscope™ is a structured, goal-based financial diagnostic that brings your life goals, financial resources, protection and future requirements into one comprehensive view—so you can see where you stand, what your goals may require and what may need to change.

Is Financial Horoscope™ related to astrology?

No. It is not astrology or fortune-telling. The name is a brand metaphor for a comprehensive view of your financial life and your future requirements, based on your actual information and stated assumptions.

Who should get one?

Families, salaried professionals, business owners, pre-retirees and retirees, defence personnel, doctors, bankers and finance professionals, and HNIs—anyone who wants greater clarity about whether their financial journey is on course.

How long does it take?

The online Quick Scan takes only a few minutes. The time needed for the complete Financial Horoscope™ depends on how quickly your information is shared and how complex your situation is; we will tell you the expected timeline when you begin.

What information will I need to provide?

Family and life-stage information, income and expenses, assets and liabilities, existing investments and protection, and your important goals and aspirations. You share only what you choose to, through the designated workflow.

Is my information confidential?

Yes—confidentiality is a core principle of the Financial Horoscope™ journey. Your information is used only for preparing and discussing your Financial Horoscope™ and related services, and access is limited to legitimate operational purposes. See our Privacy Policy.

Does Financial Horoscope™ recommend financial products?

Financial Horoscope™ begins with your life and goals, not with products. Where you choose to act on your roadmap, we may discuss suitable products that we distribute as AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributors (and in other registered capacities, where applicable). The final decision is always yours, after reading the relevant scheme or offer documents.

Are future values or projections guaranteed?

No. Future values, inflation and expected-return assumptions are estimates used for planning. They are not guarantees, and actual outcomes can differ.

How often should my Financial Horoscope™ be reviewed?

Periodically, and whenever your life, income, goals, markets or other important circumstances change materially.
